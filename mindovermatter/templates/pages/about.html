About us

Mind Over Matter is a student-run organization that makes psychology research legible to the people it's about.

What we do

We host talks, panels, and workshops on psychology and the research behind it. Sessions are led by students, faculty guests, and researchers who are happy to explain their work to a room that isn't full of specialists.

Research credit

At campuses where we're approved, attending an event and passing a short quiz earns SONA research credit. The quiz opens after the event and asks for the attendance code announced at the end of the session. Credit reaches your account after an officer verifies your submission.

High school chapters

Our high school chapters run talks and discussion sessions only. We don't collect quiz submissions or personal information from high school students, and no credit is attached to those events.

Get involved

We're always looking for speakers, chapter officers, and schools that want to start a chapter. Send us a message.
