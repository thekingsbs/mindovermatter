Privacy

What we collect, why, and how long we keep it.

What we collect

When you submit a quiz we store your name, email address, school identifier, your answers, and your score. We collect this only to route research credit to the right account.

Who sees it

Chapter officers at your own school, and the participant pool coordinator in your psychology department when credit is granted. Officers at other chapters cannot see your submission.

How long we keep it

Submissions are deleted at the end of the academic year after credit has been granted.

High school students

We do not collect quiz submissions or personal information from students at our high school chapters.

Removing your data

Email us through the contact page and we'll delete your submission.

This page is a starting point written for a student organization. Have someone at your school review it before you launch.
