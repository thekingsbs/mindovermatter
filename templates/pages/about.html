About us

Mind Over Matter is a student-run psychology workshop at nearby colleges and partner high schools.

We started because psychology should be for everyone, not just those of us who study it for our future career. Our presentations take real psychological science and apply it to real life experiences. We hope that eventually this knowledge can be accessible to everyone, and maybe we can change the world around us.
